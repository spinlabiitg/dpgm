Three pandal-hopping routes starting from IIT Guwahati. Tap a stop to find it on the map, or open the whole route in Google Maps for live traffic.

2026 highlightBishnupur Sarbajanin: a floating pandal on a "Naglok" theme, reached by boat. Expect a 2–3 hour wait in the free queue. I couldn't confirm the exact spot from public maps, so it has no pin. Search for it in Google Maps.

Leaving IITG: the Saraighat bridge and the Jalukbari–Maligaon stretch jam badly after 5 pm. Leave in the afternoon, or plan to come back late at night.

Pins: solid pins are on a mapped landmark. Dashed pins mark the centre of the neighbourhood; once you're there, follow the crowd and the lights.

Sources: pandal names and themes come from 2024–26 reports in the Sentinel, NE Now, Outlook Traveller and Curlytales. Some areas are listed from general knowledge. Check each committee's page for this year's timings. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
